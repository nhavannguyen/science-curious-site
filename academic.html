DEEP DIVE

Clinical Biochemistry & Enzymology

Investigating enzyme kinetics, metabolic pathways, and structural biochemistry to optimize clinical diagnostic pathways at Denison University. My goal is to apply quantitative and computational tools to understand molecular mechanisms of disease.

🔬 Core Research Topics

Enzyme kinetics & inhibition mechanisms

Analyzing rate equations, binding affinities ($K_m, V_{max}$), and types of inhibition (competitive, non-competitive, allosteric). Utilizing computational modeling and spectroscopy to decipher complex allosteric interactions and understand how mutations disrupt normal protein function in genetic diseases.

Metabolic pathway integration

Studying how major metabolic pathways (glycolysis, TCA cycle, fatty acid oxidation) are regulated and connected. Understanding the biochemical basis of metabolic disorders like diabetes, where dysregulation leads to severe clinical consequences.

Computational structural biology

Applying tools like PyMOL and molecular dynamics simulations to visualize protein-ligand binding and analyze conformational changes. Using bioinformatics to predict the impact of clinical variants on protein structure and stability, bridging the gap between genomic data and structural function.

🛠️ Key Techniques

  • UV-Vis Spectroscopy
  • Enzyme Assays (Michaelis-Menten)
  • Molecular Dynamics Simulations
  • Bioinformatics (Sequence/Structure)
  • Protein Purification

Future Direction

Interested in leveraging High-Throughput Screening (HTS) to identify novel small molecule inhibitors for therapeutically relevant enzymes. Exploring the direct-to-PhD pathway in clinical biochemistry.
